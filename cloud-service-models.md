# 1. Cloud Service Models

## Definition

These are the three main cloud service models.

- **IaaS** — Infrastructure as a Service
- **PaaS** — Platform as a Service
- **SaaS** — Software as a Service

## Why?

They **define** how much **infrastructure** Microsoft manages for us.

## Example

- **IaaS** → Azure VM
- **PaaS** → Azure App Service
- **SaaS** → Microsoft 365

## When to use?

- **IaaS:** You **need** maximum control.
- **PaaS:** You **want** to deploy applications without managing servers.
- **SaaS:** You simply want to use ready-made software.

# 📘 CRM

**CRM means Customer Relationship Management.**

It is software used by companies to manage customers and customer-related activities.

## Examples of CRM

- **Salesforce**
- **Microsoft Dynamics 365**
- **HubSpot**
- **Zoho CRM**

## What does a CRM do?

A CRM can help a company:

- **Store customer information**
- **Track sales leads**
- **Manage customer interactions**
- **Track sales opportunities**
- **Handle customer support**
