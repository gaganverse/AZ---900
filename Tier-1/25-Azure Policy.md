# 25. Azure Policy

## Definition

It's a governance service that **enforces organizational rules and standards**.

## Why?

It ensures resources comply with organizational requirements.

**Comply** — Obey rules.

## Example

**Example 1:**

Company rule:

Only deploy resources in approved regions.

Azure Policy can enforce this.

**Example 2:**

Every resource must have a required tag.

## When to Use

Use Azure Policy for:

- **Compliance**
- **Governance**
- **Standards**
- **Restrictions**

## ⭐ Important Difference

**RBAC** → **Who can do something?**

**Policy** → **What is allowed/required?**
